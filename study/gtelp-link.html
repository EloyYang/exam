지텔프 문법 연결어 공식과 연습 문제

지텔프 Level 2 · 문법

공식

앞뒤 문장의 관계를 보세요.
  • 반대: however, nevertheless, on the other hand
  • 결과: therefore, as a result, consequently, thus
  • 추가: moreover, in addition, furthermore, besides
  • 예시: for example, for instance / 대신: instead / 그동안: meanwhile
  • 접속사: although, even though, unless, because, while, as soon as

연습 문제 11개

  1. Students at the university love the new cafeteria because of its wide selection. The restaurant's food is excellent. ____, its prices are far too high for most students.

    1. Therefore
    2. However
    3. For example
    4. Similarly
    정답과 해설

    (b) However — 좋은 점 ↔ 나쁜 점 반대 → However

  2. A powerful snowstorm hit the northern provinces on Monday night, and workers struggled to clear the highways. The heavy snow blocked all the roads. ____, the school was closed for two days.

    1. As a result
    2. Nevertheless
    3. Instead
    4. Meanwhile
    정답과 해설

    (a) As a result — 원인 → 결과 → As a result

  3. Peter had hoped to get his driver's license before the summer vacation started. ____ he studied hard for months, Peter failed the driving test again.

    1. Because
    2. Unless
    3. Although
    4. As soon as
    정답과 해설

    (c) Although — 열심히 했는데 떨어짐 → 양보 → Although

  4. A fitness magazine recently published an article about the health benefits of daily walking. Regular exercise improves your heart health. ____, it can reduce stress and help you sleep better.

    1. However
    2. Moreover
    3. Otherwise
    4. Instead
    정답과 해설

    (b) Moreover — 장점에 장점 추가 → Moreover

  5. Tech reviewers have been praising the latest smartphone from Orion Electronics. The new phone has a great camera. ____, its battery lasts for two full days.

    1. However
    2. In addition
    3. Otherwise
    4. Instead
    정답과 해설

    (b) In addition — 좋은 점 + 좋은 점 → 추가 In addition

  6. A naturalist gave a talk on how animals adapt to different environments around the world. Many animals are active during the day. Owls, ____, hunt mainly at night.

    1. therefore
    2. on the other hand
    3. for example
    4. moreover
    정답과 해설

    (b) on the other hand — 낮에 활동 ↔ 밤에 사냥 → 대조 on the other hand

  7. The Rangers and the Warriors had been looking forward to the match for weeks. ____ it was raining heavily, the soccer match continued as planned.

    1. Because
    2. Even though
    3. Unless
    4. So that
    정답과 해설

    (b) Even though — 비가 왔지만 경기 계속 → 양보 Even though

  8. An usher at the Grand Hall explained the entrance rules to the waiting crowd. You will not be allowed to enter the hall ____ you show your ticket.

    1. because
    2. unless
    3. although
    4. since
    정답과 해설

    (b) unless — 표를 보여 주지 않으면 → unless (= if not)

  9. A popular band announced a farewell tour, and tickets went on sale on Monday morning. The concert tickets sold out in an hour. ____, the organizers added a second show.

    1. However
    2. As a result
    3. For example
    4. Otherwise
    정답과 해설

    (b) As a result — 매진 → 공연 추가 → 결과 As a result

  10. Tom's teachers describe him as a shy student who rarely raises his hand. Tom is quiet in class. ____, he becomes very talkative with his close friends.

    1. Therefore
    2. On the other hand
    3. In addition
    4. Likewise
    정답과 해설

    (b) On the other hand — 수업 중 조용 ↔ 친구들과는 수다 → 대조 On the other hand

  11. A bus driver warned a group of tourists who were enjoying their last evening in town. We should leave early ____ we miss the last bus home.

    1. although
    2. or else
    3. so that
    4. because
    정답과 해설

    (b) or else — 일찍 안 나가면 막차를 놓친다 → 그렇지 않으면 or else
