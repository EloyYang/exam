지텔프 문법 조동사 공식과 연습 문제

지텔프 Level 2 · 문법

공식

문맥으로 고르세요.
  • can 능력·가능 / could 과거 능력·약한 추측
  • must 의무·강한 확신 / should 충고·당위
  • may/might 추측·허락 / will 의지·미래

연습 문제 12개

  1. The city has passed a new traffic regulation to reduce accidents among motorcyclists. You ____ wear a helmet when riding a motorcycle. It is required by law.

    1. must
    2. might
    3. could
    4. would
    정답과 해설

    (a) must — 법으로 요구되는 의무 → must

  2. Mina and her friends planned a hike, but the weather suddenly changed as they reached the trail. The sky is getting dark. It ____ rain later this afternoon, so take an umbrella just in case.

    1. must
    2. may
    3. shall
    4. can't
    정답과 해설

    (b) may — 확실하지 않은 추측 → may

  3. Anna moved to Paris after college and now works for a French fashion magazine. Since she has lived in Paris for ten years, Anna ____ speak French fluently.

    1. can
    2. should
    3. might not
    4. shall
    정답과 해설

    (a) can — 능력 → can

  4. Mr. Evans has worked overtime for three weeks to finish a big project, and his coworkers are worried. You look exhausted. You ____ take a few days off to rest.

    1. should
    2. can't
    3. would
    4. might
    정답과 해설

    (a) should — 충고 → should

  5. The National Museum has put up signs near its most valuable paintings and statues. Visitors ____ not touch the paintings. Security guards will stop anyone who does.

    1. must
    2. can
    3. would
    4. might
    정답과 해설

    (a) must — 금지·규칙 → must not

  6. Tony failed his first driving test by a narrow margin and feels discouraged. His instructor told him that with a little more practice, he ____ pass the test easily next time.

    1. must
    2. could
    3. should not
    4. would not
    정답과 해설

    (b) could — 가능성 → could

  7. Jason's team has been waiting for his response about the schedule since lunchtime. Jason isn't answering his phone. He ____ be in a meeting, but I'm not sure.

    1. must
    2. might
    3. should
    4. will
    정답과 해설

    (b) might — 확실하지 않은 추측 (I'm not sure) → might

  8. The mathematics teacher announced the rules for next week's exam during class. Students ____ bring a calculator to the math exam, but it is not required.

    1. must
    2. may
    3. should
    4. will
    정답과 해설

    (b) may — 허락 (필수는 아님) → may

  9. A traffic officer warned drivers on a busy street downtown. You ____ park here. It's a fire lane, and you will be fined.

    1. must not
    2. don't have to
    3. might
    4. would
    정답과 해설

    (a) must not — 금지 → must not

  10. Amy and her friends are planning a night of stargazing on the hill. The sky is clear tonight. We ____ be able to see the stars from the hill.

    1. can't
    2. should
    3. must not
    4. won't
    정답과 해설

    (b) should — 기대·예상 → should

  11. Mia, a professional swimmer, recalls her early years in the pool. When I was a child, I ____ swim, but now I swim every day.

    1. can't
    2. couldn't
    3. must
    4. shouldn't
    정답과 해설

    (b) couldn't — 과거 능력의 부정 → couldn't

  12. The Municipal Art Museum has recently announced a new policy for all visitors. Since the museum is free, visitors ____ pay for admission.

    1. must
    2. don't have to
    3. should
    4. can't
    정답과 해설

    (b) don't have to — 무료 → 낼 필요 없다 don't have to
