지텔프 문법 시제 (진행·완료진행) 공식과 연습 문제

지텔프 Level 2 · 문법

공식

지텔프 시제는 진행형이 정답인 경우가 대부분이에요. 시간 표현을 찾으세요.
  • right now, currently, at the moment → 현재진행 am/is/are -ing
  • when + 과거 동사, at that time, last ~ → 과거진행 was/were -ing
  • next ~, tomorrow, when + 현재 동사 → 미래진행 will be -ing
  • for + 기간 + now / since → 현재완료진행 have been -ing
  • for + 기간 + before / when + 과거 → 과거완료진행 had been -ing
  • by the time + 현재, by + 미래 시점 + for 기간 → 미래완료진행 will have been -ing

연습 문제 28개

  1. Kevin's manager called the apartment to ask about a missing file. His roommate answered and explained that Kevin can't come to the phone right now because he ____ a shower after his morning run.

    1. takes
    2. is taking
    3. has taken
    4. will take
    정답과 해설

    (b) is taking — right now → 현재 진행 중인 동작 → 현재진행 is taking

  2. The final exam at Westbrook High School was running smoothly until a loud noise echoed through the building. When the fire alarm went off, the students ____ their final exam in the main hall.

    1. were taking
    2. take
    3. have taken
    4. will be taking
    정답과 해설

    (a) were taking — When + 과거(went off) 순간에 하고 있던 일 → 과거진행 were taking

  3. Lisa Moreno is a bank clerk who recently received a promotion from the head office. Lisa ____ at the downtown branch for six years before she was transferred to Seoul last month.

    1. has been working
    2. had been working
    3. is working
    4. will have worked
    정답과 해설

    (b) had been working — for six years before + 과거(was transferred) → 과거 이전부터 계속 → 과거완료진행 had been working

  4. The city council has been planning a modern public library near the river for years. By the time the new library opens next spring, the city ____ on the project for three years.

    1. will have been working
    2. has been working

    4. is working
    정답과 해설

    (a) will have been working — By the time + 현재(opens) + for 기간 → 미래완료진행 will have been working
