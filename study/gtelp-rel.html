지텔프 문법 관계사 공식과 연습 문제

지텔프 Level 2 · 문법

공식

  • 사람 → who(주격) / whom(목적격) / whose(소유)
  • 사물 → which / that (단, 콤마 뒤 계속적 용법에는 that 불가)
  • 장소 → where, 시간 → when (뒤에 완전한 문장)
  • 선행사가 사람인지 사물인지, 빈칸 뒤가 완전한지 보세요

연습 문제 11개

  1. The Biology Department announced a special guest lecture for next month. The scientist ____ discovered the new species will give a lecture at our university.

    1. which
    2. who
    3. where
    4. whose
    정답과 해설

    (b) who — 선행사 사람 + 주어 빠짐 → who

  2. Tourists visiting the old town often ask about the history of its famous landmarks. The Grand Hotel, ____ was built in 1920, is the oldest building in the city.

    1. that
    2. which
    3. who
    4. where
    정답과 해설

    (b) which — 사물 + 콤마(계속적 용법) → which (that 불가)

  3. Mr. Lee took his children to the countryside for the weekend and showed them the old family home. This is the village ____ my father grew up.

    2. that
    3. where
    4. who
    정답과 해설

    (c) where — 장소 + 뒤에 완전한 문장 → where
