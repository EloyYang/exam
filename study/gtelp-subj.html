지텔프 문법 당위성 (should 생략) 공식과 연습 문제

지텔프 Level 2 · 문법

공식

요구·주장·제안·명령 동사(insist, demand, suggest, recommend, require, ask, request, propose, urge, advise)나 필수·중요 형용사(important, essential, necessary, vital, crucial, imperative, mandatory) 뒤의 that절에는 동사원형이 와요. (3인칭이어도 -s 없음, be동사는 be)

연습 문제 12개

  1. The Harmon Factory had two serious accidents last year, so the manager announced new rules. The manager insisted that every employee ____ the safety training by Friday.

    1. completes
    2. complete
    3. completed
    4. will complete
    정답과 해설

    (b) complete — insist that → 주어 + 동사원형 → complete

  2. Mrs. Ito was admitted to the hospital after a bad infection and is now recovering. The doctor said it is essential that the patient ____ this medicine twice a day.

    1. take
    2. takes
    3. took
    4. is taking
    정답과 해설

    (a) take — essential that → 동사원형 take

  3. Sam is a talented student, but his teacher noticed that foreign listeners often misunderstand him. The teacher recommended that Sam ____ more attention to his pronunciation.

    1. paid
    2. pays
    3. pay
    4. will pay
    정답과 해설

    (c) pay — recommend that → 동사원형 pay

  4. The scholarship office received hundreds of applications this year, and several were incomplete. It is important that the documents ____ submitted before the deadline.

    2. be
    3. were
    4. will be
    정답과 해설

    (b) be — important that → be동사 원형 be
